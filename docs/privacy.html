Privacy Policy

Protyp app · Last updated: 27 November 2026 (English version added)

Protyp (package name com.protyp.app) is an app that explains DIY hobby projects you can make at home step by step, with a materials list and store prices. This page explains what information the app handles. If this English text and the Turkish version differ, the Turkish version applies.

In short

  • No account is needed; we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • If the app crashes, an error report without personal information is sent so we can fix the bug (see: Crash reports below).
  • Your favorites and shopping list are stored only on your phone; they are not sent to us.

What is stored on your phone

  • Favorites and shopping list: the projects you picked and the materials you checked.
  • "I have it" marks and completed steps: materials you marked as already at home and steps you checked off in projects.
  • Language setting: the app language you chose (Turkish, English or phone language).
  • First-launch notice: the date you confirmed reading the notice.
  • Price cache: a copy of the last downloaded price file, so prices can be shown without an internet connection.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it.

What the internet connection is used for

  • Price file: Current prices are downloaded from a file in a public GitHub repository (raw.githubusercontent.com). We send nothing that identifies you with this request; however, as with any internet request, GitHub can technically see your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Video thumbnails: Thumbnail images of project videos are loaded from YouTube servers (i.ytimg.com).
  • External links: When you tap "Watch on YouTube" or a store price, the page opens in the YouTube app, your browser or the store's app. From that point on, that service's own privacy policy applies (YouTube/Google, Trendyol, Hepsiburada, etc.).

Crash reports

When an error or crash happens, the release version of the app sends a technical report to the Sentry service. The report contains only: the error message and where in the code it happened, the app version, phone model, Android version, and the app steps just before the error (such as which screen was opened).

  • Information such as name, email, location, favorites or shopping list is not sent.
  • Your IP address is not attached to the report; no screenshots or session recordings are taken.
  • Reports are used only to fix bugs and are kept for at most 90 days.

Store links

Prices are for information only; purchases are not made in the app but on the store's own website. In the future some links may be affiliate links; in that case the store can see that you came from that link. These links cost you nothing extra. If this changes, this page will be updated.

Children

The app also includes projects suitable for children and collects no personal data from any age group. Projects involving cutting tools, heat or chemicals are marked in the app as requiring adult supervision.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only. It does not request camera, location, contacts, microphone or storage permissions.

Changes

If new features such as accounts, cloud backup or barcode scanning are added, this policy will be updated before the app update and its date will be changed.
